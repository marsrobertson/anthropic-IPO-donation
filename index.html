Gaianism

Full-stack civilisation

Gaianism treats the living Earth as the thing worth serving. We buy land and build the whole package on it, in a strict order of priority.

1 · Water

Springs, wells, catchments, wetlands. No water, nothing else works.

2 · Food

Agriculture: working farmland, orchards and gardens that feed the people on the land.

3 · Warmth

Heat and fuel, including woodland managed for firewood.

Land commons

Land bought once and held forever. Used and tended, never flipped.

Forestry

Reforestation, natural regeneration and long-term woodland stewardship.

Housing

Homes for the people who steward the land, starting with agricultural housing.

Healthcare

Care for the people living on and around the commons.

Education

Teaching land skills and everything built on top of them.

The mechanism

Shares in, land out

All of the money goes towards land. The land is held in the forever commons, owned by Gaia.

STEP 1

Pledge

Tell us how many Anthropic shares, or what value, you intend to give. A pledge is a public statement of intent, not a transfer.

STEP 2

Transfer

Once your shares are liquid and unrestricted, and a receiving entity is named on this page, you donate the shares or the proceeds.

STEP 3

Buy land

Each purchase is announced in public first, under the optimistic trust rule below.

STEP 4

Forever commons

The land is stewarded for water, food and warmth, and is not resold.

Governance

Optimistic trust

Announce what you are going to do. If nobody objects within 24 hours, you have consent to do it.

That is the whole process. It is simple, it is transparent, and it scales, because nobody waits on a committee. An objection pauses the action until it is resolved in the open.

We fund on retroactive value: act first on announced intentions, and let the results be judged and rewarded afterwards.
