Gaianism

We help restore the Earth

Gaianism treats the living Earth as the thing worth serving. In practice that means a strict order of priorities.

💧

1 · Water

Springs, wells, catchments, wetlands. No water, nothing else works.

🌾

2 · Food

Working farmland, orchards and gardens that feed the people living on the land.

🔥

3 · Warmth

Shelter and heat: agricultural housing, fuel, woodland managed for firewood.

🌲

Reforestation

Planting and natural regeneration on degraded land.

🧑‍🌾

Land stewardship

People on the land long-term, looking after soil, water and trees.

The mechanism

Shares in, land out

All of the money goes towards land. The land is owned by Gaia and held in the forever commons: used and tended, never flipped.

STEP 1

Pledge

Tell us how many Anthropic shares, or what value, you intend to give. A pledge is a public statement of intent, not a transfer.

STEP 2

Transfer

Once your shares are liquid and unrestricted, you donate the shares or the proceeds to the appropriate Gaia entity.

STEP 3

Buy land

Each purchase is announced in public first, under the optimistic trust rule below.

STEP 4

Forever commons

Title sits with Gaia. The land is stewarded for water, food and warmth, and is not resold.

Governance

Optimistic trust

Announce what you are going to do. If nobody objects within 24 hours, you have consent to do it.

That is the whole process. It is simple, it is transparent, and it scales, because nobody waits on a committee. An objection pauses the action until it is resolved in the open.

We fund on retroactive value: act first on announced intentions, and let the results be judged and rewarded afterwards.
